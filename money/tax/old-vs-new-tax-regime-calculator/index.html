How to use this calculator

  1. Choose your age group and enter your yearly gross income, which is your income before any deductions.
  2. Tick the salaried box if you earn a salary or pension. This adds the standard deduction.
  3. Enter your 80C investments, health insurance and home loan interest using the boxes or sliders. Open "More options" for HRA, NPS, parents' insurance and other deductions.
  4. Read the verdict, then the breakeven. Use "Copy Result" to save or share the summary, and "Reset" to start again.

How the calculation works

The calculator works out your taxable income and tax separately for each regime, then compares them.

Taxable income = gross income − deductions allowed in that regime
Tax = slab tax − rebate, plus surcharge (if any), plus 4% health and education cess

Worked example: ₹15,00,000 salary, new regime

Taxable income is ₹15,00,000 − ₹75,000 standard deduction = ₹14,25,000. Tax is ₹20,000 (₹4 to 8 lakh at 5%) + ₹40,000 (₹8 to 12 lakh at 10%) + ₹33,750 (₹12 to 14.25 lakh at 15%) = ₹93,750. Adding 4% cess of ₹3,750 gives ₹97,500.

Rebate, standard deduction, surcharge and cess

  • Standard deduction for salaried people and pensioners: ₹75,000 in the new regime and ₹50,000 in the old regime.
  • Rebate: new regime tax is nil up to ₹12 lakh of taxable income (rebate up to ₹60,000), with marginal relief just above it. Old regime tax is nil up to ₹5 lakh (rebate up to ₹12,500).
  • Surcharge: 10% above ₹50 lakh, 15% above ₹1 crore and 25% above ₹2 crore. Above ₹5 crore it is 37% in the old regime and stays at 25% in the new regime. Marginal relief is applied at each step.
  • Cess: 4% on tax plus surcharge, in both regimes.

Old regime deduction limits used here

  • 80C: up to ₹1,50,000.
  • Health insurance (80D): ₹25,000 for self and family (₹50,000 if you are a senior citizen), plus ₹25,000 for parents (₹50,000 if they are seniors).
  • Extra NPS, 80CCD(1B): up to ₹50,000.
  • Home loan interest on a self-occupied house: up to ₹2,00,000.
  • HRA: the lowest of HRA received, rent paid minus 10% of basic pay, and 50% (metro) or 40% of basic pay.
  • Employer NPS: allowed in both regimes, up to 14% of basic pay in the new regime and 10% in the old.

How the breakeven is found

If the old regime costs more, the calculator keeps adding deductions to it, step by step, until its tax matches the new regime. The total deductions at that point is your breakeven. It then shows how much of the gap you could close using the room left in each limit. If the old regime already wins, it shows how far your deductions could fall before that changes.

What is not included

Capital gains and other special-rate income, alternative minimum tax, foreign income, and less common deductions. Use the "Other old-regime deductions" box for any old-regime deduction not listed. This is an estimate for planning, not a filed return.

Frequently asked questions

Is income up to ₹12 lakh really tax-free in the new regime?

Under the new regime, taxable income up to ₹12 lakh gets a rebate that brings the tax to nil. A salaried person also gets a ₹75,000 standard deduction, so gross salary up to ₹12.75 lakh can end up with no tax. Income taxed at special rates, such as capital gains, is not covered by this calculator.

Which regime is better for me, old or new?

The new regime has lower rates and almost no deductions. The old regime has higher rates but lets you subtract deductions such as 80C, HRA, health insurance and home loan interest. The old regime only wins when your total deductions are high enough. The breakeven figure in this calculator shows exactly how high they need to be.

Can I switch between the old and new regime every year?

Salaried people with no business income can generally choose a regime each year when they file their return. People with business or professional income face stricter rules on switching, so check with a chartered accountant. In every case, file your return by the due date.

Is my income data stored or sent anywhere?

No. Every calculation runs in your browser on your own device. Nothing you type into the calculator is uploaded or saved.
