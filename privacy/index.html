Privacy Policy

How MyOmniKit handles data, browser processing, and advertising.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Client-Side Tool Data

All calculators and converters on MyOmniKit.com run locally inside your web browser. We do not collect, transmit, or store the income figures, health inputs, dates, or files you enter into any tool.

2. Information You Send Us

If you contact us via email at hello@myomnikit.com, we receive your email address and message content solely to reply to your inquiry.

3. Advertising and Cookies (Google AdSense)

We may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense to support free access to our tools. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to MyOmniKit.com and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.
  • For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, consent preferences are managed through a Google-certified Consent Management Platform before personalized ads load.

4. Server Logs

Our hosting infrastructure may record standard technical request logs (such as IP address, browser type, and referring URL) strictly for security, abuse prevention, and uptime monitoring.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@myomnikit.com.
